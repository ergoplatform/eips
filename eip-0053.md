# EIP-0053: Storage Rent Grace Period

* Author: kushti
* Status: Proposed
* Created: 26-Sep-2026
* License: CC0
* Forking: Soft Fork (activation on predefined height by the majority of mining hashrate, no block version change)

## Motivation

Under the original rules, an expired box whose value does not cover the storage fee (`box.value - storageFee <= 0`) may be fully consumed by anyone immediately after the storage period (4 years) ends. For a plain value box that is fine, as it is about lost coins recirculate and the UTXO set is cleaned. But many boxes carry tokens or additional registers whose contents matter to parties other than the (possibly absent) owner: oracle and governance tokens, data registers of long-lived contracts, etc. Destruction of such a box destroys assets and data that others still depend on, with no chance to rescue them.

## Specification

Starting from the activation height **H**, for any expired box **holding tokens or additional registers** the spending rules change in two ways. First, on recreation the box may be charged only *towards the minimum allowed value*: the recreated output's value must be at least `max(box.value - storageFee, minValue)`, where `storageFee = storageFeeFactor * box.bytes.length` and `minValue = minValuePerByte * box.bytes.length` (all registers except R0/R3 and the script must be preserved, as before). Second, the box may be *fully consumed* under the original condition (`box.value - storageFee <= 0`) only after a **grace period** of 10,080 blocks (2 weeks) has passed since the box became rent-eligible, i.e. when `currentHeight >= box.creationHeight + StoragePeriod + StorageGracePeriod`. During the grace period anyone may recreate the box, after which its storage clock restarts. Boxes without tokens and additional registers are unaffected: they are charged and consumed under the original rules, so UTXO dust cleanup is unchanged.

## Soft-Fork Safety and Activation

The change is a tightening of the original rules: every spend valid under the new rules is also valid under the original ones, so it can be enforced by the majority of mining hashrate without a new block version — updated miners simply never include the newly-invalid claim shapes in their candidates. Activation is by flag day at height **H** (mainnet value to be finalized before the release). The original rules are applied verbatim below **H**, so all historical blocks keep validating on resync, and non-updated nodes continue to accept blocks produced by updated miners.
